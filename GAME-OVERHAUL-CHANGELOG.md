# NIGHTFALL MIX 5.0 · Six nights, one mix

## Play and progression

- Choose one of three starting styles: Heart Relay connects invitations, Neon Guard builds protection through charging, and Bounce Rider rewards controlled flight.
- Earn run-only XP through road actions, then pause to choose one of three offered perks. Nine distinct perks support up to six drafts per tour; repeated actions against the same opportunity cannot farm XP.
- Build family synergies add another decision: focus a style or combine strengths. The existing four-slot workshop remains separate, with nine current-car modules and one purchase per stop. A fifth installed module requires explicit replacement.
- Overlapping perks and workshop modules combine meaningfully: extra piercing and recycling add together, paired chain upgrades reach two nearby targets, and paired rush-wave upgrades extend their range. Upgrade descriptions explain these combinations.
- Seeded encounter phrases preserve readable guards, moving crowds, committed dashes and safe recovery spaces. City headliner encounters are fictional speaker signals with readable response phases, not people the player must defeat. They remain optional to clear.
- Charging, invitation/combo and careful-driving goals retain their seeded opportunity limits. EV routes favor recovery; event and service routes offer different rewards and risks. Retry restores the departure checkpoint, including run progression, without farming failed-road rewards.

## Story and dialogue

- Fifty authored scenes give the twelve-road tour a continuous goal: record six city soundchecks and join them into the Kyoto finale mix.
- Each city has a street road and a club road. Intercity transfers and rest happen between tour nights; Tokyo-to-Kansai travel is explicitly separate from the short playable roads.
- Arinee's measured driving decisions and Chana's playful navigation develop through departures, arrivals, retries and the finale. All clubs remain fictional game locations.
- Hearts are invitations. People respond voluntarily and can join in their own style; the story no longer frames them as identities to transform.
- An opening choice about people or discovery changes later dialogue, with no hidden stat or currency reward. Optional dialogue and a plain-text journal preserve story continuity without blocking the run.

## Presentation and access

- A rebuilt tour title, loadout selection, mastery display, perk drafts, story scenes and journal sit alongside the route map, arrival receipt and build workshop.
- Help and persistent controls have their own space outside the active road. Contextual action guidance, next-EV distance, resource warnings and headliner status support decisions during driving.
- Auto-hearts, assist mode and reduced motion are persisted independently of narrative memory and the gameplay checkpoint. Reduced motion retains static action and hazard feedback while suppressing moving effects.
- All four driving actions remain available by keyboard and touch. Story and draft scenes pause play, and scene transitions clear held actions. Jump still escapes held charging safely.
- Original detailed district, vehicle, duo, umbrella and console artwork is preserved. The optional eight-phrase final DJ encore uses the existing four actions, after completion points have been secured.

## Preserved contracts

- Twelve playable legs across six cities, direct destination-node choices and all existing illustrated districts/seasons.
- Car unlocks/selection remain new-run-only; ordinary tour coins fund current-car improvements.
- One persistent SoundCloud iframe, native player access without a sound-menu prerequisite, and no scene-transition remount.
- World/crew animation with a reduced-motion option, Kansai Japanese copy, deterministic 120 Hz simulation and checkpoint migration.
- No new runtime dependency, telemetry, account, payment flow or deployment credential. Existing optional-audio protections remain intact.
