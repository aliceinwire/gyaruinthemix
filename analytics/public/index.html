What these numbers mean

Visitors are estimated from a daily rotating hash of the client address, browser family, and device category. Repeat visits on the same day count once; the same person on different days counts again. Shared networks can undercount, and changing networks or browsers can overcount.

Reloads add page requests; browser-cached pages may not reach the server. Known bots, assets, redirects, checkout returns, and this dashboard are excluded. Some bots may still appear.

“Direct / unknown” includes apps, internal links, and browsers that hide referrers. These figures do not measure music streams, podcast listeners, sales, or link clicks.

No analytics cookies or browser storage are used. Raw IP addresses, query strings, and full referrer URLs are not saved by analytics. Daily counts and daily visitor hashes are retained for 90 days.
